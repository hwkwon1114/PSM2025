### Outcome-independent dome criterion (freeze before any search)

Reference-material central region: |u|<=80 mm, |v|<=120 mm, with face-centroid inclusion and reference-area weights. Report whole-sheet distributions separately, including edges; do not hide failures outside the central region. A qualifying dome requires at least 80% of central reference area to have principal curvatures of a common sign (either both positive or both negative, with one consistent surface normal), weaker magnitude >=0.1 m^-1 and weaker/stronger magnitude ratio >=0.1. This is an operational qualitative-dome threshold, not experimental accuracy or a statistical confidence claim. The 0.1 m^-1 floor corresponds to approximately 0.32 mm sag over a 160 mm chord in small-slope constant curvature. Report sensitivity at floors .05/.2 m^-1 and coverage thresholds 70/90%, without changing the primary criterion after viewing results. Reject an apparent dome supported only by isolated cell noise, folds or a local bump; use spatial maps, full unexaggerated shape and a geometry-based local fit cross-check. No independent-replicate interpretation of mesh vertices.

Geometry-derived principal curvature from full-precision native endpoint positions is the primary acceptance measurement. Exported H/K use director-dependent b and are a separate confirmation diagnostic, not geometric proof. Compute coverage separately for each globally coherent sign; require one sign to cover 80%, never pool convex and concave patches. Invalid fits remain in the fixed ROI denominator and contribute zero qualifying area. If geometric and director-based classifications disagree, qualification is unresolved, not success. Retain spatial/unexaggerated-shape vetoes.

Freeze the geometry estimator on analytic fixtures before viewing simulation outcomes: local quadratic Euclidean tangent-chart fits, consistently oriented by mesh connectivity, using first and second fundamental forms rather than a reference-coordinate height Hessian. Use native JSON positions, not float32 VTP points. Record neighborhood scale, support and conditioning rules after analytic plane, sphere/dome, cylinder and saddle checks with rigid transformations. Reconstruct director principal values from H/K in SI units using scale-aware discriminant checks and stable smaller-root recovery; invalid/nonfinite fields cannot pass. Do not use the unverified legacy principal-direction export. A global quadratic fit is diagnostic only.
