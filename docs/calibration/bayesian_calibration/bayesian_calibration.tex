\section*{Bayesian calibration}
Bayesian optimization (BO) was used to calibrate the growth and orthotropy parameters, denoted by $\boldsymbol{\theta}$, by minimizing the discrepancy between the simulated sheet and the experimental point cloud. For each parameter proposal, the simulation produced deformed mesh vertices $\mathbf{x}_j(\boldsymbol{\theta})$ corresponding to reference vertices $\mathbf{X}_j$, with $j=1,\ldots,M$.

Rigid-body rotation was removed by fitting the deformed vertices to their material reference:
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{R}_{\boldsymbol{\theta}}=
\underset{\mathbf{R}\in SO(3)}{\operatorname{arg\,min}}
\sum_{j=1}^{M}\left\|
\mathbf{R}[\mathbf{x}_j(\boldsymbol{\theta})-\bar{\mathbf{x}}(\boldsymbol{\theta})]
-(\mathbf{X}_j-\bar{\mathbf{X}})\right\|_2^2.
\end{equation}
Here bars denote arithmetic vertex means. The aligned simulation coordinates were defined as
\begin{equation}
\widehat{\mathbf{x}}_j(\boldsymbol{\theta})=
\mathbf{R}_{\boldsymbol{\theta}}\left[\mathbf{x}_j(\boldsymbol{\theta})-
\sum_{k=1}^{M}w_k\mathbf{x}_k(\boldsymbol{\theta})\right],
\qquad w_j=\frac{A_j}{\sum_{k=1}^{M}A_k},
\end{equation}
where $A_j$ is one-third of the total reference area of triangles incident on vertex $j$. Experimental scan points $\mathbf{y}_i$, $i=1,\ldots,N$, were centered as
\begin{equation}
\widehat{\mathbf{y}}_i=\mathbf{y}_i-\frac{1}{N}\sum_{\ell=1}^{N}\mathbf{y}_{\ell}.
\end{equation}
No scan-fitted rotation, scaling, or reflection was applied.

BO minimized the bidirectional root-mean-square shape discrepancy
\begin{equation}
L(\boldsymbol{\theta})=
\left\{\frac{1}{2}\left[
\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}d(\widehat{\mathbf{y}}_i,\widehat{S}_{\boldsymbol{\theta}})^2
+\sum_{j=1}^{M}w_j\min_{1\leq i\leq N}
\left\|\widehat{\mathbf{x}}_j(\boldsymbol{\theta})-\widehat{\mathbf{y}}_i\right\|_2^2
\right]\right\}^{1/2},
\end{equation}
where $\widehat{S}_{\boldsymbol{\theta}}$ is the triangular surface formed by the aligned simulated vertices and
\begin{equation}
d(\mathbf{y},S)=\min_{\mathbf{s}\in S}\|\mathbf{y}-\mathbf{s}\|_2.
\end{equation}
The two contributions respectively measure scan-to-surface and reference-area-weighted simulation-to-scan distances.

Parameter settings were proposed within prescribed bounds using previous evaluations to guide subsequent proposals. Failed simulations were not assigned valid shape-discrepancy scores.
