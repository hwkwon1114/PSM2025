# Conditional sensitivity of the historical Run 1 objective

One-dimensional slices of a post-hoc Matérn-5/2 Gaussian process fitted to 221 valid archived observations; other parameters are fixed at Attempt 209. Curves are the back-transformed log-objective predictive median; shading is the pointwise 95% latent-GP interval conditional on fitted kernel hyperparameters, not a parameter confidence interval. Rugs show locations of all valid observations projected onto each parameter axis, not observations made on the displayed slice. The two growth coordinates are logarithmic; orthotropy coordinates are linear. The fit combines adaptively sampled records, including 80 rescored prior observations and later records from changing search stages. It models the historical material-reference objective and earlier numerical convention, not the corrected-convention shared-footprint scores used in the shape figures. A chronological check trained on valid IDs 1–200 and predicted valid IDs 201–240; these are adaptive, not independent validation. Smoothness and bands do not establish causal sensitivity, uniqueness, or physical identifiability.
