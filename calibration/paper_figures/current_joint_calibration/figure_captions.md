# Calibration figures: draft captions and editorial notes

## Optimization history

**Figure.** Joint-calibration search history under the revised shared-footprint surface objective. (a) Earlier joint search; (b) the separate, parallel comparison arms A (success-aware) and B (failure imputation); (c) the bottom-growth extension warm-started from Arm A; (d) the final reduced search; and (e) a linear-scale detail of panel (d), showing only valid values at or below 0.70 mm. Panels (a)–(d) use the same logarithmic ordinate. Each circle or triangle represents a valid saved evaluation; open symbols identify evaluations rescored retrospectively with the revised metric. Red crosses (Arm A and other stages) and plus signs (Arm B) denote failures with no objective value, placed at the bottom of each panel only as markers. Lines give the lowest valid revised score available at the end of each batch within that stage or arm—not a single uninterrupted optimization trajectory. In particular, the retrospectively rescored portion is not necessarily the objective that guided the original proposals. The horizontal offsets and jitter separate points within each batch and do not indicate completion order.

The earlier joint search contains 48 records (29 valid, all rescored); Arm A contains 44 (33 valid, 10 rescored) and Arm B 60 (30 valid, five rescored); the extension contains 24 valid evaluations; and the final search contains 48 (44 valid, three solver failures and one timeout). The first 12 design configurations were matched between A and B and evaluated in both arms; they are not independent configurations. Arm A and B were not warm-started from the earlier search. The extension inherited Arm A's records; its batch-0 diamond shows the best valid warm-start score. The final search inherited only the 10 extension records with bottom growth exactly zero; its batch-0 diamond is their best score, 0.509 mm. The extension's overall best, 0.505 mm, had nonzero bottom growth and was therefore not eligible for the final reduced search. Imported warm records are shown at their original evaluations and are not counted again as new evaluations; the diamonds summarize their eligible best values. The final selected value was 0.488 mm. Early stopping followed the planned improvement rule and does not establish convergence.

## Conditional GP sensitivity

**Figure.** Four one-at-a-time Gaussian-process response slices for the final joint objective: (a) top-layer growth magnitude, (b) top-layer orthotropy, (c) hardening decay rate and (d) hardening floor. Each curve varies the indicated parameter while the other three are fixed at the selected candidate. Blue lines are the predicted medians of the joint RMS; shaded bands are nominal 95% latent-prediction intervals conditional on the fitted GP and its hyperparameters. The orange point is the selected actual evaluation. Gray ticks show the marginal locations of valid training observations, not observations on each slice. The plotted ranges span the observed valid marginal ranges; the growth and decay axes and all objective axes are logarithmic. The bottom-layer growth is fixed to zero in this reduced model.

**Editorial note:** The post-hoc Matérn-5/2 GP was fitted to 54 unique valid warm-start and final-stage points. It is not the optimizer's recorded acquisition model and does not include a failure-probability curve. The points were adaptively selected and are dependent. A time-ordered check trained on the eligible warm records and waves 1–8 and predicted 16 valid points from waves 9–12: RMSE in log objective was 0.231 and 15/16 were within the nominal 95% intervals. This is a limited valid-only diagnostic, not an independent validation or proof of interval calibration. The bands are **not parameter confidence intervals**, and one-at-a-time slices do not account for compensating changes in other parameters or establish identifiability.

## Full-field fit and error distribution

**Figure.** Measured and predicted sheet shapes for Run 1 and Run 3, used in the joint calibration, and Run 2, an exploratory frozen-parameter check. Rows show (a) experimental height, (b) numerical shell-model height (labelled Analytical), (c) signed height difference Δy = Analytical − Experiment at matched plan-view coordinates, and (d) the full distribution of scan-to-model surface distances. Vertical lines in (d) give the scan-to-model RMS and 95th percentile. The two height maps share one color scale across all cases; the signed residual maps share another. Plan-view axes are x (width) and z (length), and y is height. Scan triangles longer than 5 mm are omitted to avoid bridging gaps. All retained scan points are supported by the predicted surface at their plan-view locations. In Run 2, 26 of 14,468 signed residual values exceed the displayed ±2.5 mm color range and are saturated; their distances remain included in the histogram and metrics. The plotted distance distribution is one direction of the two-sided optimization metric, and Δy is not a Euclidean distance.

**Editorial note:** Run 2 was not used in the joint objective but its earlier results were known, it was inspected during the campaign, and a longer-time-limit retry followed an initial timeout. It must not be described as pristine, unseen, one-shot validation. All three rows are one scan and one forward calculation; spatial points within a sheet are not independent experimental replicates.

## Matched cross-sections

**Figure.** Matched x–y sections at z = −35, 65 and 165 mm, selected before examining the residuals. Solid orange curves with markers show the experiment and dashed blue curves the numerical shell-model prediction; black curves beneath each section show Δy = Analytical − Experiment. Sections use piecewise-linear interpolation on a 0.5 mm x grid; unsupported coordinates are left as gaps. The same height and residual scales are used for all runs. Run 1 and Run 3 were fitted jointly; Run 2 is the exploratory check described above. “Analytical” denotes the numerical model, not a closed-form solution.

## Provenance and labels

These captions use the researcher-selected public labels. The archived file identifiers remain unchanged for traceability: public Run 1 = historical `run6`, public Run 2 = historical `run9`, and public Run 3 = historical `run10`. The input hashes, exact selected parameters, metrics, GP settings, predictive-check results, and display limits are recorded in `figure_data_and_provenance.json` in this directory. The figures use saved solves only; no new simulation was run to create them.
