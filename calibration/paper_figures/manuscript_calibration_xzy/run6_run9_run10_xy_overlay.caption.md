Whole-sheet x–y projections of Run 6 calibration and Runs 9/10 frozen-parameter transfers, viewed along the longitudinal z direction. Experiment is a directionally shaded piecewise-linear scan triangulation; Analytical is a wire grid interpolated on native model facets. Scan triangles with XZ edges >5 mm are excluded. Both datasets use the same 12× vertical exaggeration and shared physical axis limits; tick values remain in mm. Brightness encodes lighting, not error. All z positions overlap: these are not individual sections. Wires deliberately render over the experimental surface, so wire visibility does not imply positive residual. No new registration, smoothing fit or simulation. Fixed-z sections and residual maps provide quantitative comparisons.
