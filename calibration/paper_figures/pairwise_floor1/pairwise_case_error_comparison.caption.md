# Frozen pairwise fit errors on all three cases

Each row is the minimum valid joint-RMS fit of the indicated calibration pair; each column is a public run. Filled circles show the two calibration-case symmetric surface RMS errors, and open circles show the one excluded-case exploratory transfer after parameters were frozen. Cross and plus show the 95th percentile of scan-to-model and model-to-scan unsigned distances, respectively, with small vertical offsets only for legibility. The p95 values are unweighted quantiles of the respective sampled distances; the reverse RMS uses reference-area weighting. Spatial distances are dependent. All runs were previously seen, so excluded cases are not blind validation. No error bar or independent-sample uncertainty is implied. See pairwise_provenance.json and pairwise_results.csv for identities, selection rules, candidate failures, and other observations.
