# Width–height projections of the pairwise calibrated shapes

Rows are frozen fits to Runs 1+2, 2+3 and 1+3; columns are Runs 1, 2 and 3. The excluded-run panels are exploratory checks, not blind validation. Orange points are experimental scan points; blue lines are the saved simulation mesh grid. This is an orthographic x–y projection across the entire length z, not a section at fixed z; overlapping points can belong to different lengths and are not pointwise matches. The x and y axes use unequal display scales. All panels share the same limits. The 2,500 points per case are the identical fixed-seed sample used in the 3-D companion figure, not selected by error; model grid lines are piecewise-linear on saved facets and masked outside the mesh. Titles give the full three-dimensional shared-footprint symmetric surface RMS in mm, not an error of this 2-D projection or the displayed point sample. No new registration, solver or refitting was performed. Data are spatially dependent. Full directional error distributions appear in pairwise_case_error_comparison.
