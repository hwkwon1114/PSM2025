# Cross-sections for Run 6 (calibration) and Runs 9 and 10 (transfer)

Experiment (orange, markers every 16 mm) and Analytical (blue, dashed) heights y along fixed lines z = −35, 65 and +165 mm (x–y sections; z is sheet length), chosen in advance and not selected by error; rows are Run 6, Run 9 and Run 10. Lower strips show Δy = Analytical − Experiment. Axis limits are shared by all panels. Horizontal and vertical scales differ, so slopes are visually exaggerated.

Sections are sampled every 0.5 mm. Experiment heights are linearly interpolated on the scan triangulation (excluding triangles with an edge > 5 mm), and Analytical heights on the predicted shell facets; neither is smoothed or fitted, and gaps are left unfilled. Same frozen Attempt 209 parameters and evaluation as the full-field figure; one run per row.
